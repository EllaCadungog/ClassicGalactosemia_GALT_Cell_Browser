### Why This Dataset Was Selected?

The selected human tissue was chosen because the GALT gene encodes galactose-1-phosphate uridylyltransferase, an enzyme involved in galactose metabolism. GALT functions in the Leloir pathway, where it catalyzes the transfer of a uridylyl group from UDP-glucose to galactose-1-phosphate, producing UDP-galactose and glucose-1-phosphate. GALT is important for normal galactose metabolism, and reduced or absent GALT activity is associated with Classic Galactosemia.
